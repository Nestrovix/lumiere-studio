The studio

We photograph the ten seconds
nobody thought to pose for.

Fourteen years, six hundred stories, one approach: arrive early, stay quiet, wait for the frame. Our daylight studio handles the controlled work — portraits, still life, campaigns — and the rest of the year we are on location, from a farmhouse in Provence to a launch floor in Singapore.

Every commission is shot by a named photographer, graded by hand, and delivered in a private gallery you keep for life.
