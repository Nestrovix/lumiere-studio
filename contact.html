Before you write

Questions we
get most weeks.

How far in advance should we book?

Weddings are usually booked nine to eighteen months out. Commercial and portrait work is often possible within two to three weeks.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel within the tri-state area is included; beyond that we quote flights and accommodation at cost, with no markup.

How long until we see the images?

A same-night preview of eight to twelve frames, a proof gallery within five days, and the full hand-graded set within fifteen.

Who owns the photographs?

You receive full personal usage rights on delivery. Commercial clients receive the licence agreed in the quote, worldwide and in perpetuity unless stated otherwise.
