Wedding Photography

Full-day documentary coverage with a second shooter, an editorial eye and none of the posing that makes people look like strangers.

  • Ten to twelve hours of coverage
  • Second photographer included
  • Engagement session
  • 500+ retouched images in a private gallery
  • Optional fine-art album

Fashion & Editorial

Campaign, lookbook and magazine work. We build the treatment with your creative director, then shoot it in the studio or wherever the light is right.

  • Concept development and mood boards
  • Casting, styling and HMU coordination
  • Studio or location production
  • High-end retouching to print standard
  • Full commercial usage licensing
Investment From $3,200 / day

Portrait Photography

Headshots, personal branding and fine-art portraiture. One person, one hour, one look that actually sounds like you.

  • 60 or 90 minute sitting

  • Wardrobe consultation beforehand
  • Same-week proof gallery
  • Retouched files sized for print and web
Investment From $650

Commercial & Product

Still life, packshots and brand imagery shot on a colour-managed set, delivered ready for e-commerce, print and paid media.

Event Photography

How it works

From first email
to final frame.

01

Enquiry

Tell us the date, the place and what matters. We reply within one working day with availability and a fixed quote.

02

Treatment

A short document: references, light, wardrobe, shot list and schedule. Nothing goes to camera before we agree on it.

03

The shoot

One named lead photographer, an assistant, and as little interference as the day allows.

04

Delivery

Hand-graded selects in a private gallery within five to fifteen days, with print and web files and full usage rights.
